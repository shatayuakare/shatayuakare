## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Full Stack MERN and React Native Developer, currently working as a **Developer and Digital Manager at Security Plus Electronics**. In this dual-capacity role, I bridge core software engineering with digital strategy—building responsive web applications with React and WordPress, managing end-to-end e-commerce integrations, and executing high-impact digital marketing campaigns and performance ads. My primary focus lies in architecting scalable platforms such as the **CCTV Mall Corporate Platform** and the **Rento Go Car & Bike Rental Platform**, ensuring seamless back-end logic, clean API design, and intuitive user experiences.

Beyond day-to-day development, I actively collaborate on open-source ecosystems, modern full-stack web products, and cross-platform mobile applications. I hold a strong foundation across **JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS, and relational databases**, and I am continuously deepening my expertise through my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program while exploring advanced cross-platform architectures with **React Native**. My technical edge comes from pairing deep full-stack engineering with real-world business acumen—delivering products that are not just technically sound, but commercially viable, scalable, and optimized for audience conversion.
